## E · Dependence on Experimental Results

**Score**: 2; scored
**Criterion**: Real experimental results serve as formal testing; performance can be independently evaluated on experimental data. 
**Rationale**: The four groups of FOM labels come from high-throughput synthesis and electrochemical experiments. The paper explicitly computes test-set MAE on unmeasured compositions and separately reports test-set MAE results. Round-by-round selection is offline simulation on these measured labels; this does not change the fact that the testing basis is real experimental results.
**Level comparison**: Level 0 does not fit because the labels are indeed obtained from real synthesis and electrochemical experiments. Level 1 is also insufficient to describe the formal unmeasured-sample test and separately reported test-set MAE; therefore level 2 is adopted.
**Task**: T-seq
**Boundary & dispute notes**: "simulated SL" might be mistaken for "only proxy labels". The source also explicitly states the complete labels come from experiments, and uses these labels to evaluate test error on unmeasured compositions.
