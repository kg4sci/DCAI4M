## U · Uncertainty & Failure Handling

**Score**: 3; scored
**Criterion**: Noise, failures, and negative results are explicitly handled. 
**Rationale**: Formal evaluation iteratively corrects too-close atoms in generated structures, rejects structures that cannot be corrected or fail relaxation, and reports failure proportions; the alloy branch also stipulates rejection conditions for failed structures. The minimum and maximum of three independent trainings are only run variability, not proving predictive uncertainty guides screening.
**Level comparison**: U0 does not fit the explicit correction, rejection, and failure reporting; U1's run range does not equal predictive uncertainty; the uncertainty-driven candidate screening or risk search required by U2 and U4 is not supported by these procedures; U3 fits best with the explicit noise and failure-result handling.
**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: The failures and negative results in the U3 anchor manifest in this task as failed generated structures; if real experimental negative samples were required, the score would differ, but the original U3 anchor does not restrict to real experiments.

## E · Dependence on Experimental Results

**Score**: 0; scored
**Criterion**: No real experimental results; evaluation uses only simulation or proxy labels. 
**Rationale**: T1's alloy structures come from cluster-expansion Monte Carlo, amorphous silicon from melt-quench molecular dynamics, and interface benchmark data only undergo M3GNet relaxation; model performance is evaluated by benchmark metrics between generated and training structures. The DFT interface data the paper separately uses for CryinGAN development are computational data, explicitly distinguished from T1's interface data. No real experimental measurements are found as T1's training, calibration, or formal testing basis.
**Level comparison**: Level 0 requires no real experimental results and evaluation relying only on simulation or proxy data, which the three data sources and metric protocol fit. The experimental training, calibration, or case results required by level 1, and the formal experimental testing required by level 2, both do not fit the described T1 computational data and benchmark evaluation protocol.
**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: MD structures from literature, real materials names, background experimental observations, or DFT computations were not mistaken for experimental measurement results.

**Score**: pending (candidate 0); pending (with candidate score)
**Criterion**: Insufficient information; no numeric level selected for now
**Rationale**: T1's metric comparison objects are generated structures and computational training structures. **Level comparison**: Level 0 needs proving the conditions needed to interpret experimental results are indeed missing; the paper's lack of experimental-condition narrative is insufficient for this judgment.
