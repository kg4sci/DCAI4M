## I · Workflow Integration

The next-query planning here is computational oracle evaluation planning, not interpretable as implemented physical experiments.

**Task**: T1

Level 4 is chosen based on its implemented integrated discovery workflow of generation, prediction scoring, screening, and feedback updating, and this scope of applicability must be retained in review.
## G · Goal & Constraint Handling

**Score** 3(Expert review)
**Criterion**: Multi-objective or multi-constraint optimization. 
**Rationale**: The formal objective is maximizing the number of new stable materials within finite queries. Candidates are actually subject to multiple static constraints such as allowed elements and maximum stoichiometry, validated before structure generation; the query budget also limits decisions. The paper lists true multi-objective tasks as future work, and post-hoc diversity metrics cannot be treated as joint optimization.
**Level comparison**: Level 1's single discovery objective is clear, and level 2's static constraints are also clear; at least two types of runtime constraints, elements and stoichiometry, satisfy level 3's multi-constraint branch. Level 4 requires dynamic, multi-objective, and resource-constrained; this paper lists multi-objective tasks as a future direction.
**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: The search space and budget are formal protocol constraints; specific element and stoichiometry validation is explicitly implemented in the evaluated LLM planner. Level-3 capability applies to that strategy and limited task scope, and does not equal dynamic multi-objective optimization.

**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: The original anchor mentions noise, failures, and negative results together, while the evidence mainly targets the latter two; this judgment treats it as explicit failure-handling capability without claiming noise modeling. This interpretation needs focused checking during the Independent review.
