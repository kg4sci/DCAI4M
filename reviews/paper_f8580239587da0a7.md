**Level comparison**: Level 3's "Candidate generation, inverse design, or goal-directed design." is satisfied by O-1; O-2 further proves that screening results decide the next DFT candidate, so level 4 is adopted. Other models' background experiment-planning capability is not counted.
**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: If "next step" is limited to physical experiments or a new generation round after feedback, only level 3 can be taken; the original anchor also explicitly covers next candidates, and the source has proven the decision of candidates entering DFT, so level 4 is taken.

## I · Workflow Integration

**Score** 3(Expert review)
**Criterion**: Multiple models or modules connected in sequence. 
**Rationale**: In T1, the crystal generative model's output enters CHGNet stability prediction and screening, and those predicted stable then enter DFT verification. **Level comparison**: Level 2 can describe the enhanced version of a single task plus screening; level 3 is more accurate, because the generative model and CHGNet prediction module are actually connected in sequence, followed by DFT verification. **Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: The number of multiple software components itself is not scored; I-1 and I-2 show the actual connection where task-level generation results enter prediction screening and then validation.

**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: Initial candidate 0 emphasizes point prediction and the lack of candidate-level uncertainty; Independent review candidate 3 emphasizes the source's false-positive penalty. **Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: Experimental structures are a model training source, not this task's experimental stability labels; historical experimental results can support level 1, but the evidence scope of a single optional baseline must be retained during review.

**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: Level 1 reflects a direct link to synthesis needs; DFT stability cannot be equated with full synthesizability, nor can R and E be demoted together merely because actual synthesis tests are lacking.
