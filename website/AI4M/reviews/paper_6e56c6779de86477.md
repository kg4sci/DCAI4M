**Criterion**: Real experimental results serve as formal testing; performance can be independently evaluated on experimental data. 
**Rationale**: Real historical thermochemical measurements are the basis of formal accuracy evaluation, and performance can be read separately on the experimental subset. **Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: Independent review candidate 2 worries that actinide-oxide estimates are mixed into the aggregate; this concern holds for the overall set, but E-4 explicitly lists 278 binary intermetallic compounds separately, and the estimates listed under E-2 are PaO2/NpO3 oxides, so the two are distinguishable.
