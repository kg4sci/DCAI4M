- Paper ID: paper_5e6dcc9758a01aa2
- Paper title: Cost-Aware Recovery-Pathway Identification and Bayesian Optimization for Autonomous Materials Discovery
- Dataset / benchmark: CICERO-inspired synthetic benchmark (three feedstock-inspired instances: Produced water, SmCo leachate, NdFeB leachate)
- Task: given finite recycling-pathway hypotheses, priors, priced diagnostics and their likelihood models and application thresholds, sequentially selecting diagnostics to discriminate the target metal/precipitation-reagent pathway, then selecting continuous-condition batches within that pathway based on noisy simulated observations, reaching the recovery-quality target at lower cost. The three feedstocks are instances of the same whole-field strategy task, each using a purity or separation-factor scalar response; the main evaluation compares cost-to-target and the best noise-free response.
- Evaluation object: in the CICERO-inspired synthetic benchmark (three feedstock instances), the cost-diagnostic recycling-path discrimination and within-path continuous-condition sequential optimization task and its formal evaluation workflow
- Provided workflow: the authors construct synthetic response surfaces and diagnostic models constrained by CICERO's published endpoints and trends. Coactive learning selects diagnostics by expected posterior decision-region impurity reduction per dollar, updates the posterior after observing results, commits to a pathway once a confidence threshold is reached, and abstains if diagnostics are exhausted but still insufficient; then, starting from an initial plate biased toward the domain center, it repeatedly fits a GP and uses batch GP-UCB to select conditions and obtain simulated noisy responses. It formally compares Oracle-pathway, Split-plate, Commit-first BO, and random search, reports results over 20 seeds, and additionally performs ablations and a SmCo pure-optimization control.

GP-UCB also uses the predictive standard deviation for conditional selection. Thus it is not merely reporting run fluctuation or basic confidence; risk-awareness holds within the given likelihood model.
**Level comparison**: Level 2 "Uncertainty is used in candidate selection." already holds; level 4 "Uncertainty drives a risk-aware search strategy." is further supported by active impurity reduction and confidence thresholds/abstention. **Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: Risk here is limited to the risk of choosing the wrong path under a given diagnostic likelihood model, without claiming physical safety or robust risk control. The basis for 4 is posterior-driven search and a wrong-commitment threshold, not 20-seed standard deviation or merely the GP-UCB name.
