## U · Uncertainty & Failure Handling

**Score**: 2; scored
**Criterion**: Uncertainty is used in candidate selection. 
**Rationale**: GPR's predictive uncertainty and mean for each candidate jointly enter the per-property EI computation, and EI further decides the next evaluation candidate. Therefore uncertainty actually affects selection. The kernel's expected noise parameter cannot alone prove failure or negative-result handling.
**Level comparison**: Level 1 requires uncertainty not to affect the policy, opposite to EI using predictive uncertainty to select points; level 2 has direct evidence. Level 3's noise, failure, and negative-result handling cannot be inferred merely from the GPR noise parameter; level 4's risk-aware search is also not supported by the current method description.
**Task**: T1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: The expected noise parameter might make level 3 appear applicable, but the original level also involves explicit handling of failures and negative results, and the existing citation is insufficient to extend that parameter into a task-level failure mechanism.
