Level 2 does not imply the existence of experimentally measured continuous property labels.
**Task**: T-1
**Boundary & dispute notes**: Independent candidate 1 emphasizes there are no measured decomposition-energy labels; but the original anchor allows historical experimental results as formal testing basis, and this paper's Technical Validation explicitly forms a group of 59 grown materials and reports their computational energies and coverage limitations, so this item is level 2, qualified as positive-case validation.
